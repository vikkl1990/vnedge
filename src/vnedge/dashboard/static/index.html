Autonomous Perps Desk
VN EDGE

A trading desk that hunts for edge — and refuses to risk your capital finding it.

VN Edge watches BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and DOGE across Binance, Bybit and Delta India, and runs a fleet of scanners that read the market on every bar. It holds only shadow and paper positions. It never places a live order until a strategy earns it — through governed, human-gated promotion on data it has never seen.

Principle 01

Capital protection beats profit

The first rule is not losing the money. Position size comes from risk-per-trade and stop distance — never from leverage — and a fixed daily-loss halt ends the day before a bad one can compound.

Principle 02

Deny by default

Every scanner is suppressed until it proves a real, fee-aware edge on untouched data. Most never do — and that is the point. The fee wall is the honest judge; permutation tests catch the lucky.

Principle 03

Shadow before live

A strict ladder — backtest → paper → shadow → live — where each rung is validated before the next. The live order path stays sealed behind three independent locks and never opens on its own.

Principle 04

Always explainable

Every rejection carries every failed check, not just the first. No silent failures, no hidden state, no default-on live paths. The machine shows its work — so you can trust what it does and does not do.
